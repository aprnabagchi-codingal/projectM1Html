Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam

The Missile Man of India

Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam was born on 15 October 1931 in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu. He was a great scientist, teacher and the 11th President of India.

Early Life and Education

He came from a humble family and worked hard to achieve his dreams. He studied aerospace engineering and became a famous scientist.

Achievements

He played an important role in India's space and missile development programmes. He received the Bharat Ratna, India's highest civilian award.

Famous Quote

"Dream, dream, dream. Dreams transform into thoughts and thoughts result in action."

Why I Admire Him

I admire Dr. Kalam because he was hardworking, honest and dedicated to his country. He inspires students to dream big and work hard.
