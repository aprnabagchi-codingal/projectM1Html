Healthy Food

Importance of Healthy Eating

Healthy food gives our body the energy and nutrients needed to stay active. Fruits, vegetables, milk, nuts and grains are important parts of a balanced diet.

Nutrition Tips

Five Healthy Eating Habits

  1. Eat fresh fruits and vegetables.
  2. Drink enough water.
  3. Eat a balanced breakfast.
  4. Avoid too much junk food.
  5. Eat meals at regular times.

Benefits of Drinking Water

Water helps regulate body temperature, supports digestion and keeps the body hydrated.

Did You Know?

Eating a variety of colourful fruits and vegetables helps provide different vitamins and minerals.
